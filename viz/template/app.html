Misfit vs depth

Source depth is essentially unobservable from two cables at similar depth. Measured across 32 real calls, the best misfit varies by a median of just 0.3% over the whole −200…0 m range (worst case 1.0%). That is why every published method for this dataset fixes source depth rather than estimating it — the reference implementation's 176 solutions all carry z = −30 m exactly.
